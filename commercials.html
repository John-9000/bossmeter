Boss Commercials

A brief commercial intermission for your boss journey

Boss Commercials are optional advertising moments connected to the Boss Meter experience. The goal is to support the free version of Boss Meter without interrupting the main score roll, boss tier results, history, or inspiration features.

Advertisement

Instead of placing advertisements everywhere, Boss Meter keeps them in a dedicated commercials area. This means users can enjoy the main Boss Meter experience first, then choose whether they want to visit the commercials page.

What this page is for:

  • Showing optional advertisements in a clearly labeled area
  • Explaining how Boss Meter uses ads to support the free version
  • Giving users a simple way to return to the main Boss Meter page
  • Keeping advertising separate from the main score experience

Why Boss Meter uses commercials

Boss Meter is designed as a lightweight entertainment app. Advertising helps keep the basic experience available while allowing optional premium features for users who want more themes, extended history, custom timers, and an ad-free option.

Ads on this page may be provided by third-party advertising services. Depending on your region and privacy choices, ads may be basic or personalized. Personalized ads may use more data to make ads more relevant, while basic ads use less personalization.

Your choice matters

Boss Meter aims to keep advertising clear and user-controlled. The advertisement area is labeled, the page includes navigation back to the main experience, and privacy information is available from the footer.

Quick questions

Are commercials required?
No. The main Boss Meter page can be used without staying on the commercials page.

Why is the advertisement labeled?
The label makes it clear when content is advertising, so users can easily understand what they are viewing.

Where can I learn more about privacy?
You can read the Boss Meter privacy policy using the footer link at the bottom of this page.

About Boss Meter app

Boss Meter is a fun, lightweight mobile app created for entertainment.

It generates a randomized "boss level" score designed to be playful and motivational, not a scientific, psychological, or professional assessment. Refined ad experience - no interruptions, even in the free version ads appear only when you choose, and in the premium version ads can be removed completely.

Free version includes:

  • Boss score rolls & tier system
  • 2 Boss themes (Classic, Gold)
  • History
  • Boss Commercials (non-intrusive)
  • Timer-end notifications

Premium version unlocks:

  • 6 additional Boss themes (White, Purple, Silver, Midnight, Ocean, Azure)
  • Instant roll & custom roll timer
  • Extended history
  • Ad-free option & premium Boss visuals
