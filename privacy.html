Boss Meter Google Play App Privacy Policy

Boss Meter is an entertainment application that generates a score for fun and sharing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Boss Meter does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

The app generates a score locally on the user's device. This score is not stored on any external server.

2. Advertising

Boss Meter contains no advertisements and does not include advertising SDKs. The app does not access the advertising identifier or collect data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Boss Meter does not store personal data on external servers. Any settings or generated scores remain locally on the user's device.

Achievement progress also stays on the device. It includes roll counts and patterns, scores and tiers reached, themes used, local play dates, and milestones for opening Inspiration and History or using Share Score.

App settings, scores, history, achievements, and earned theme unlocks are excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfers. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Premium purchases are managed by Google Play and can be restored using the Google account that made the purchase. Restoring Premium does not restore app settings, scores, history, achievements, or earned progress.

4. Third-Party Services

Boss Meter may use the following third-party services:

Google Play Billing – used to process and restore Premium purchases.

Google Play in-app updates – used to check for available app updates.

These services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Boss Meter is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this document.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact:

support@unrstudio.com

Website Privacy Policy (bossmeter.app)

The Boss Meter website and app contain no advertisements. The website does not load advertising services, use advertising identifiers, or request advertising consent.

BossMeter.app uses local storage, session storage, and a functional cookie to remember your last Boss roll time, most recent score, and Boss Inspiration state. The Inspiration cookie lasts up to seven days. This browser data supports the score and Inspiration features and is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

You can remove this locally stored website data by clearing cookies and site data in your browser settings. Doing so resets saved website results, timers, and Inspiration state.

If you choose to share a Boss Score, its score and verification value are included in the shared link. Anyone with the link can view that score.

The website links to external services such as Google Play. When you follow an external link, that service processes information under its own privacy policy.
